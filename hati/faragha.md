# Sera ya Faragha ya Pachisi / Pachisi Privacy Policy

Kajara Studio -- ilisasishwa 2026-11-25

## Kiswahili

Pachisi haikusanyi, haihifadhi kwenye seva, wala haiuzi taarifa zako. Hakuna
akaunti, hakuna matangazo, hakuna takwimu za matumizi.

- **Wasifu na rekodi**: jina ulilochagua, kitambulisho cha mchezaji (KJ-XXXX-XXXX)
  na rekodi za michezo huhifadhiwa kwenye simu yako pekee. Ukiifuta programu,
  vinafutika.
- **Vyumba vya Wi-Fi**: ukiunda au kujiunga na chumba, jina lako la mchezaji na
  hatua za mchezo hutumwa moja kwa moja kwa simu za wachezaji wengine kwenye
  mtandao uleule wa Wi-Fi au hotspot. Hazipiti kwenye seva yoyote.
- **Kipaza sauti**: hutumika tu ukiwasha kipaza ndani ya chumba. Sauti yako
  hutumwa moja kwa moja kwa simu za wachezaji wa chumba hicho wakati huo, na
  haihifadhiwi popote. Unaweza kuzima kipaza wakati wowote.
- **Watoto**: Pachisi haikusudiwi kukusanya taarifa za watoto, na haikusanyi
  taarifa za mtu yeyote.

Maswali: fungua suala kwenye https://github.com/lugha-swa/pachisi/issues

## English

Pachisi does not collect, store on any server, or sell your information. There
are no accounts, no ads and no usage analytics.

- **Profile and records**: the name you choose, your player ID (KJ-XXXX-XXXX)
  and your game records are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app
  deletes them.
- **Wi-Fi rooms**: when you host or join a room, your player name and game moves
  are sent directly to the other players' phones on the same Wi-Fi network or
  hotspot. They do not pass through any server.
- **Microphone**: used only when you turn the microphone on inside a room. Your
  voice is streamed directly to the phones of the players in that room at that
  moment and is never recorded or stored. You can turn the microphone off at
  any time.
- **Children**: Pachisi does not collect information from anyone, including
  children.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/lugha-swa/pachisi/issues
